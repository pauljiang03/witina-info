Information used by the app

witina is a chat assistant for tasks, calendars, morning briefs, check-ins and preparation for upcoming work. Its server stores messages and replies; tasks, reminders and local events; an optional profile called witina.md; and a dated ledger of recorded preferences, claims, commitments, decisions and their evidence and corrections. Separate, tentative assistant notes expire after 1–30 days. A ledger records what was said or changed; it is not independent proof that something happened.

The account system uses Sign in with Apple. It stores your Apple identifier, optional name and email, hashed session credentials and encrypted Apple authorization credentials. The app also uses notification tokens, delivery records, settings, consent choices, usage and spending counts, submitted feedback and summarized crash or hang diagnostics. Voice transcription uses on-device Apple speech recognition when available; the app sends transcribed text, not microphone audio, to its server.

AI is your choice

Before AI assistance is enabled, the app asks permission to send messages, relevant tasks, calendar details, personalization and saved context to Anthropic, the Claude API provider. These support replies, briefs, check-ins, preparation and saved context, including background assistance while the app is closed. The app does not train or fine-tune a general model on your account. AI responses can be wrong.

Anthropic's applicable API retention terms will be confirmed for the deployed account before the hosted beta opens. See Anthropic's API retention information. This notice does not claim zero provider retention.

Public research is a separate opt-in setting and is off by default for beta accounts. When enabled, public topics derived from requests, tasks or events are sent to Anthropic's search service. The complete conversation and profile are omitted from the research request, and known identifiers and sensitive strings are screened out. Screening cannot guarantee that a topic reveals no private context. The server may read selected public pages to obtain supporting information; those sites receive requests from the server, including the requested URL. Page text is treated as untrusted evidence, and research is not automatically recorded as confirmed personal facts.

Turning AI assistance off stops new AI processing. A request already sent cannot be recalled. Separate settings control proactive categories, quiet hours and notification previews.

Calendars and notifications

Google, Outlook and feed imports are disabled for the initial friends beta. Local events remain inside witina and do not change external calendars or send invitations. Any later calendar-enabled release will update these disclosures before activation. Disabling imports does not itself erase older development data or revoke existing grants.

Apple delivers push notifications. Notification text is generic by default; if you enable text previews, a message excerpt is included. A webhook you choose receives proactive message text. Use a destination you trust.

Access, retention and deletion

Settings lets you export account data, edit witina.md and delete your account. You can ask the assistant to correct or stop using a recorded claim. Corrections preserve ledger history. Clearing chat history deletes conversations and their briefs from the active database, including queued notifications, but leaves ledger evidence, assistant notes, preferences, tasks and events. Account deletion removes account data and credentials from the active database; unattributed spending records remain to enforce the service's spending ceiling.

Saved account content otherwise remains until account deletion. Expired assistant notes leave active context immediately and are removed by scheduled cleanup. Notification deliveries expire after 24 hours, and terminal delivery records are pruned after seven days. Reports you explicitly submit may include the response excerpt shown before submission; they are included in account export and removed from the active database by account deletion.

Before the hosted beta opens, this page will name the actual infrastructure providers and verified backup retention period, and explain how deleted accounts are excluded after a restore. We do not promise immediate erasure from backups. The service is not end-to-end encrypted against its operator: infrastructure operators can technically access stored content.
