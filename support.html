Beta availability

Elori is in development. A shared TestFlight beta has not opened yet. TestFlight invitations will explain which features are available in that build.

Tell us what happened

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. If you have a TestFlight build, you can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

Share a screenshot or response only if you want us to see its contents. Please leave out passwords, API keys and private calendar links.

Your information

In the app, Settings provides account export, AI controls and account deletion. Clearing chat history removes the conversation; saved preferences, ledger evidence, notes, tasks and events remain. Use account deletion to erase your account from the active service.

If you cannot access your account, contact the address above. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before acting on a request.

Calendar scope

The initial friends beta is planned to support tasks and events added inside Elori. Google and Outlook imports will be unavailable in that build. Events added in Elori do not change your Google or Outlook calendar or send invitations.
